## BNS -- Murder
Definition
Murder is the intentional causing of the death of another person under
circumstances that satisfy the legal ingredients of the offence.
Investigation Focus
-   Motive
-   Murder weapon
-   DNA evidence
-   Fingerprints
-   CCTV footage
-   Witness statements
Keywords
Murder Homicide Weapon Forensics DNA
## BNS -- Theft
Definition
Dishonestly taking movable property from another person's possession
without consent.
Investigation Focus
-   Ownership documents
-   CCTV footage
-   Recovery of stolen property
-   Fingerprints
Keywords
Theft Vehicle Theft Property Crime

## BNS -- Robbery
Definition
Robbery involves theft accompanied by violence or intimidation.
Investigation Focus
-   Weapon used
-   Injuries
-   Witness identification
-   CCTV footage
Keyword
Robbery Violence

## BNSS -- FIR Registration
Purpose
Procedure for registering a First Information Report.
Important Points
-   Register FIR for cognizable offences.
-   Zero FIR is permitted.
-   Investigation begins after registration.
Keywords
FIR Registration
