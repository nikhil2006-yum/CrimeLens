# Vehicle Theft Investigation SOP

Objective

Recover the stolen vehicle, identify offenders, and preserve evidence.

Procedure

1.  Register the FIR.
2.  Secure the crime scene.
3.  Record complainant and witness statements.
4.  Collect CCTV footage.
5.  Verify ownership and registration details.
6.  Alert patrol units and nearby police stations.
7.  Check ANPR/RTO databases.
8.  Recover the vehicle.
9.  Arrest the accused.
10. File the final report or charge sheet.

Keywords

Vehicle Theft Recovery Investigation CCTV

## Cyber Crime Investigation SOP

Objective

Investigate online fraud, phishing, OTP scams, and digital offences.

Procedure

1.  Register the complaint.
2.  Preserve digital evidence.
3.  Freeze beneficiary bank accounts.
4.  Obtain server, IP, and transaction logs.
5.  Coordinate with the Cyber Cell.
6.  Perform forensic examination.
7.  Identify suspects and file charge sheet.

Keywords

Cyber Crime Digital Evidence Fraud OTP Scam

## Murder Investigation SOP

Objective

Conduct a scientific investigation while preserving evidence.

Procedure

1.  Secure the crime scene.
2.  Prevent contamination.
3.  Photograph and document the scene.
4.  Recover the weapon.
5.  Collect DNA, fingerprints, and biological samples.
6.  Record witness statements.
7.  Conduct post-mortem coordination.
8.  Arrest suspect where evidence exists.
9.  Submit forensic reports.
10. File charge sheet.

Keywords

Murder Crime Scene DNA Forensics

## Missing Person Investigation SOP

Procedure

1.  Register complaint immediately.
2.  Broadcast alerts.
3.  Check CCTV and transport hubs.
4.  Trace mobile phone location.
5.  Contact relatives and associates.
6.  Search hospitals and shelters.
7.  Coordinate with neighboring districts.
8.  Update complainant regularly.

Keywords

Missing Person Search Tracing

## Domestic Violence Investigation SOP

Procedure

1.  Ensure victim safety.
2.  Arrange medical examination.
3.  Record victim statement.
4.  Photograph injuries.
5.  Collect witness statements.
6.  Arrest accused where legally required.
7.  Provide legal and support services.

Keywords

Domestic Violence Victim Protection Medical Evidence

## Drug Trafficking Investigation SOP

Procedure

1.  Secure seizure location.
2.  Document seized narcotics.
3.  Collect fingerprints and DNA where applicable.
4.  Send samples for forensic analysis.
5.  Trace supply chain and financial links.
6.  Arrest suspects.
7.  File prosecution report.

Keywords

Drug Trafficking Narcotics Forensics

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Financial Fraud Investigation SOP

Procedure

1.  Register FIR.
2.  Freeze suspicious accounts.
3.  Collect bank records.
4.  Trace money trail.
5.  Examine digital devices.
6.  Coordinate with financial institutions.
7.  Prepare evidence for prosecution.

Keywords

Financial Fraud Money Trail Bank Records
