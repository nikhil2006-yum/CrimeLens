# Vehicle Theft Trend -- Bengaluru
Observation
Most vehicle thefts occur between 11:00 PM and 4:00 AM in commercial and
residential parking areas.

Hotspots

-   Whitefield
-   Electronic City
-   Marathahalli
-   Hebbal

Recommendations

-   Increase night patrols.
-   Expand CCTV and ANPR coverage.
-   Share stolen vehicle alerts.
